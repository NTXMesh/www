Two Meshes, One Community

Our members deploy and support both major open-source mesh firmwares — Meshtastic and MeshCore. Whichever you choose, you're part of the same North Texas community, and plenty of operators run both. We aim to share what we learn and keep both networks healthy across DFW.

About NTX Mesh

What We Do

NTX Mesh is a community-built mesh network in North Texas running both Meshtastic and MeshCore. It acts like a city-wide text messaging system — public or private — that works without cell towers, the internet, or grid power. All you need is an inexpensive mesh radio to reach others in range. Our network is built for emergency preparedness, community building, and resilient communication.

Off-Grid & Solar

Solar-powered repeaters keep the network running 24/7 — no grid power, no internet, no monthly bill.

Community Owned

Built and maintained by local volunteers across DFW. No corporations, no subscriptions, no gatekeepers.

Public & Private

Chat with the whole community on the public channel, or talk privately with friends and family on your own encrypted channel.

Configuration

Modem Presets

Note: NTX Mesh runs both Meshtastic and MeshCore, and some counties use modem presets other than the defaults (such as Meshtastic's Long_Fast) due to the localized mesh. Check the Preset Map by County and use the recommended settings for your firmware before configuring your device.
