Partners

Partner With NTX Mesh

NTX Mesh is a community group building a network of solar-powered Meshtastic and MeshCore radios across DFW and the greater North Texas area. We grow that network with help from local partners who can host infrastructure, share expertise, or open their sites to a small, self-sufficient mesh node.

Who We Work With

Partnership Types

Ham Radio Clubs

Amateur radio clubs and operators bring deep RF expertise, antenna sites, and tower access. Partnering lets us co-locate Meshtastic and MeshCore repeaters at established radio sites and grow coverage across the metroplex.

Educational Institutions

Schools, colleges, universities, and student clubs make great partners for hands-on LoRa projects, emergency-preparedness exercises, and hosting nodes on campus rooftops and structures.

Municipal Entities

Cities, water districts, and public agencies can host nodes on water towers, public buildings, and other tall infrastructure — extending resilient, off-grid communication for the whole community.

Host a Node

Open Your Site to the Mesh

If you own or manage a building, tower, or other elevated structure, you can help extend the mesh by hosting a node. Our nodes are small, weatherproof, solar-powered, and self-sufficient — typically under two pounds, with no A/C power and no cabling into the building. Mounting usually relies on magnets, u-bolts, or hose clamps, so there's no drilling or permanent modification required.
